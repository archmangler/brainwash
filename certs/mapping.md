# Certification mapping

The cert is a **phase exam**, not a third daily syllabus.

## During this C graph (weeks 1–8)

**Do not sit C++ Institute CPA/CPP yet.** That syllabus pulls classes, overloads, and the library before ownership is automatic.

Use instead:

- An **internal mock** built from `curriculum/skill-graph.md` (preferred), or
- A **C** exam (CLA / C Programming Institute style) at end of week 8 or week 12

### Mock exam (internal)

When week 4 is mostly mastered, the tutor writes a 90-minute paper:

- 10 predict-the-output items
- 3 blank-file functions (same family as katas)
- 1 short ownership / sanitizer question

Score it. Gaps become nodes or retests, not random extra reading.

## After C gates (W3.5 + W4.4 mastered)

- **C++ Institute** (CPA then CPP) becomes the next program's milestone.
- Map every official objective to a C++ skill-graph node (to be added in a later track).

## Linux Foundation

Pick an exam that matches **week 7** (POSIX / systems), or save it for a systems track. A general sysadmin LF cert does not train C fluency. Do not let it displace katas.

## Exam-window rules

Last 7–10 days before a dated exam:

- Keep the 10-minute kata and the unassisted puzzle
- Shrink the daily project
- Shift stretch time onto past papers / official objectives mapped to nodes
