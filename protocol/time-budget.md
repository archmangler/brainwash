# Time budget

The drill is inviolable. Fatigue kills more 8-week programs than difficulty.

| Day type | Drill | Puzzle | Project | Cert |
|----------|-------|--------|---------|------|
| Standard (4–5 days/week) | 60–75 min | 25–40 min | 60–90 min | 0 |
| Light (1–2 days/week) | 45 min (review + kata) | 20 min | skip or 30 min tidy | 20–30 min objectives / past Q |
| Exam window (late phase) | 45 min (review + kata) | keep | shrink | 45–60 min past papers |

If the learner only has ~2 hours: cut the project to ~45 minutes. Keep drill and puzzle.

Standard-day total is about 2.5–3.5 hours. That is already a serious load for 8 weeks.

## Sequencing in a day (suggested)

1. Drill (tutor present)
2. Puzzle (unassisted)
3. Break
4. Project (scope help only)
5. Optional light cert reading on light days
